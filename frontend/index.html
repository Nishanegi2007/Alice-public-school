A school built around the neighbourhood it serves

Alice Public School has been part of the Molarband Extension community in Badarpur, offering general education within easy reach of home.

We keep things simple: attentive teachers, a safe campus, and steady progress every term. Families are always welcome to visit before enrolling.

A message from the Principal

Every child who walks into Alice Public School arrives curious, and it is our job to keep that curiosity alive. From Nursery through Class V, we focus on the basics that matter most — reading, numbers, kindness and confidence — so each student leaves us ready for what comes next. Our doors are always open to parents, and I invite you to visit and see our classrooms for yourself.
